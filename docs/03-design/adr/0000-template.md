## Tested against
The concrete case this decision was checked against, and who supplied it — or, honestly, "none yet", naming the case that would test it and who should go looking.

A rule is not found to be wrong by re-reading it. It is found by applying it to a case its author did not have, and in P0 every such case came from work already in the tree rather than from anyone constructing one: the actor reply rule met I4's three commands, its second limit met `apply_and_wait` in the budget harness, and §3.4's truncation claim met a fixture two orders of magnitude smaller than the one it was written from. All three were widened or corrected while the cost was a conversation.

So the field exists to force the question early, while a decision is still prose. A shipped API is where the same correction becomes a breaking change and the reflex turns into contorting the caller instead of amending the rule.
