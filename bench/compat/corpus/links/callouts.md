# Callouts

> [!note]
> A plain callout with no title override.

> [!warning] Careful
> A callout with a custom title, and a second line
> of body text.

> [!tip]- Collapsed by default
> Obsidian's foldable-callout syntax (the trailing `-`). Serat renders it as a
> callout widget or as source text and never rewrites it (C-01).
